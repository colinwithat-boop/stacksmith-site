Stacksmith support

Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection tracker, for Android and iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas: stackscanapp@gmail.com. In the app, Settings > About > Contact support starts an email with the app version filled in. Replies usually come within a few days.

Scanning

  • Open the Scan tab and hold a card inside the guide, flat and filling most of it. Even light without glare on the art works best.
  • If the app asks for camera access and you said no, turn it on in your phone's settings under Stacksmith.
  • A card from a set newer than the app: open the Scan tab while online and accept the new-cards download.

Your collection

  • Everything is stored on your phone; there is no account. Settings > Backup makes a file you can keep and restore on another phone.
  • Folders can be exported as CSV from a folder's menu, in a layout other collection apps import.
