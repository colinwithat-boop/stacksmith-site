Stacksmith support

Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection tracker, for Android and iPhone.

Contact

For questions, email stackscanapp@gmail.com. To report a bug or suggest a feature, open Settings > About > Send feedback in the app. Replies usually arrive within a few days.

Scanning

  • Open Scan and hold a card flat inside the guide, filling most of the outline. Use even lighting and avoid glare on the artwork.
  • If you previously denied camera access, enable it for Stacksmith in your phone's settings.
  • For cards from a new set, open Scan while connected to the internet and download the available card update.

Your collection

  • Your collection is stored on your phone, with no account required. Open Settings > Backup to save a copy that you can restore on this phone or another one.
  • Use a folder's share menu to export a CSV file that you can import into other collection apps.
