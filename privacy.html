Stacksmith privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Stacksmith is a Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection tracker. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This page explains what the app keeps on your phone, what it sends over the internet, and when.

What stays on your phone

  • Your collection, folders, tags, wishlist, decks, price history and settings are stored in a database on your phone. We have no server, so none of it is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this database in your device backup with Google.
  • The camera is used only to recognise cards while the Scan screen is open. Frames are analysed on the phone, including the text on the card. No photos or video are stored or sent anywhere.
  • CSV export creates a file only when you ask for one, and hands it to the app you choose to share it with. Sharing your trade list or wishlist as text works the same way: the text goes only to the app you pick.
  • Backup (Settings > Backup) creates a file only when you ask for one: your collection, folders, decks, tags, wishlist, price history, price alerts, what you paid, settings and the pictures on your folders and decks. It is not encrypted. The app hands it to the app you choose to share it with, and reads a backup back only when you pick one to restore.

What the app sends over the internet, and when

Every internet request carries standard connection information, such as your IP address. Beyond that, the app sends only what is listed here.

  • Scryfall (scryfall.com): card data, set lists, card images, prices (TCGplayer's and Cardmarket's) and format legality. To refresh prices and legality, the app sends Scryfall the IDs of the cards and printings in your collection; it does not send quantities, folders or anything else about you. When you scan or open a card, or open a deck, it may fetch the current price or format legality of those cards. New card data is downloaded when you choose to.
  • tcgcsv.com: only if you choose TCGplayer Low or Mid prices in Settings. The app downloads TCGplayer's public price lists for the sets your cards come from. The only thing this reveals is which sets' lists it asks for.
  • European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu): only if you choose to show prices in a currency other than your price source's (Settings > Show prices in, or for one session in the scan screen's options, behind its gear button). The app downloads the ECB's public daily exchange rates; nothing about you or your collection is sent.
  • Commander Spellbook (commanderspellbook.com): combos.
    • When you open a deck's combos, the deck's card names and counts are sent.
    • When you open "Combos you could build", or use the deck builder or Suggest, the names and counts of the cards in your collection are sent.
    • A card's detail sheet sends that one card's name.
    • Nothing is sent to Commander Spellbook until you open one of these.
  • Archidekt (archidekt.com): Deck Search. The app sends the search you make (format, commander, card name, colours, bracket, deck name) and fetches the decks you open, including one whose Archidekt link you paste: only the deck's number from the link is sent, and only when you open it. It does not send your collection: how much of a deck you own is worked out on your phone.
  • MTGGoldfish (mtggoldfish.com): the headlines on the Home tab.
  • GitHub Pages (colinwithat-boop.github.io): card-language packs, the names (and for some languages the rules text) of cards printed in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Korean or Russian. A pack is downloaded when you add that card language in Settings, or when you pick a language whose pack is not on your phone as the one to scan cards in (in Settings, or in the scan screen's language menu, behind its language button) and agree to download it, and once on its own for your phone's language (or the app's, when you set it to one of these), unless you removed it. A pack that was installed is downloaded again on its own if an app update or a restored backup leaves it missing, and opening Settings or the scan screen's language menu fetches the list of packs and their sizes. Each request reveals your IP address and which language pack, if any, is asked for; nothing else is sent.
  • Google ML Kit, which reads the text on cards on your phone. The camera image and the text it reads are not sent anywhere. Like every app that uses ML Kit, it sends Google device information (such as the manufacturer, model and OS version), the app's name and version, a per-installation identifier, and performance and configuration details (such as how long recognition took), which Google uses for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Links you tap (to Scryfall, TCGplayer, Cardmarket, MTGGoldfish, Archidekt or Commander Spellbook) open in your browser. "Buy missing" puts the list of cards you are missing (from a deck, or from a set in your inventory) into the TCGplayer link it opens, and the wishlist's "Buy on TCGplayer" puts the cards on your wishlist (names and counts) into it.
  • Google Play: to process the optional one-time Pro purchase and to check whether it has been made. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the app never sees your payment details.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs of its own.
  • No account, sign-in, email or contacts access.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing of data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
