Stacksmith privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Stacksmith is a Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection tracker for Android. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This page explains what the app does with data.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection, folders, decks and settings are stored in a database on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The camera is used only to recognise cards while the Scan screen is open. Frames are analysed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere. Nothing is recorded when the Scan screen is closed.
  • CSV export creates a file only when you ask for one, and hands it to the app you choose to share it with.

What the app requests over the internet

The app contacts these services to fetch reference data. Requests contain the card or query being looked up and standard connection information (such as your IP address, which every internet request carries); they do not contain your collection.

  • Scryfall (scryfall.com): card data, prices, set information and card images.
  • Commander Spellbook (commanderspellbook.com): combo information.
  • MTGGoldfish (mtggoldfish.com): headlines shown on the Home tab.
  • Google Play: to process the optional one-time Pro purchase and to check whether it has been made. Google's handling of payments is covered by Google's own privacy policy; the app never sees your payment details.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, sign-in, email or contacts access.
  • No sharing or selling of data. There is no data to share: the app cannot see your collection.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
