Stacksmith privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Stacksmith is a Magic: The Gathering card scanner and collection tracker. It has no in-app accounts. This page explains what the app stores on your phone, what it sends over the internet, and when.

What stays on your phone

  • Your loose cards, sealed products, folders, tags, wishlist, decks, saved player profiles, price history and settings are stored in a database on your phone, including whether you mark a card copy as a proxy. The app does not upload this database to a Stacksmith server. You can choose to send a copy using the device-transfer or backup controls described below. Uninstalling the app deletes the local copy. If you use automatic phone backups (Android backup with Google, or iCloud Backup on an iPhone), the backup may include this database.
  • The camera is used only to recognize cards while the Scan screen is open. Frames are analyzed on the phone, including the text on the card. No photos or video are stored or sent anywhere.
  • CSV export creates a file only when you choose to export, then shares it with the app you select. Sharing your trade list or wishlist as text works the same way: the text goes only to the app you pick.
  • Backup and collection transfer create a complete collection file when you choose Backup or Send collection. It includes loose cards, sealed products, folders, decks, tags, wishlist, saved player profiles, price history, price alerts, what you paid, settings, the pictures on your folders, decks and custom themes, and custom chime sound files. The saved file is not encrypted by Stacksmith. Hidden prices and other private collection information remain in the file. Direct nearby transfer uses the encrypted connection described below. When you choose to share a file instead, Stacksmith hands it to the destination you choose in the operating system's share sheet. You can choose a nearby device with Quick Share or AirDrop, or another app or storage provider. That destination determines whether the copy travels locally or through an internet or cloud service, under its own policy. Stacksmith does not send it to a collection-transfer server.
  • Deck transfer sends the selected deck's name, format, themes, card names, quantities, printing IDs, commander flags, sideboard and Considering list in a file. It excludes your collection inventory, records of which copies are assigned to decks, purchase prices, source account details and custom photos. The saved file is not encrypted by Stacksmith. It uses the same nearby connection or file-sharing choice as collection transfer. Receiving it adds a new deck after review.
  • Folder transfer starts only when you choose Send/Receive folder for a folder and tap Send folder. It sends the folder's name and, for each card in it, the card name, set, collector number, rarity, Scryfall ID, finish, condition, language, quantity and proxy flag, directly to the nearby device you choose over the encrypted connection described below. No Stacksmith server is involved. It excludes purchase prices, tags, notes, records of cards assigned to decks and custom photos. The file is kept only in the app's private cache. When you receive a folder, you see its name and card count first. You then review the cards in Import before adding them as a new folder.
  • Direct nearby transfer starts only when you tap a send or receive button in Send/Receive app contents, Send/Receive decks or Send/Receive folder, or This is the main device or This device receives in Sync device. It uses Google's Nearby Connections SDK, which supports offline connections using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No Stacksmith collection-transfer server is used. Android's SDK chooses the connection technology. Both devices confirm the same verification code before any collection, deck or folder is transferred. The connection is encrypted by the SDK. A temporary device-model label and random suffix are visible to nearby Stacksmith devices while this screen is waiting. No card names or collection details are visible during device discovery. Received data is checked in the app's private cache before review; it is not placed in Downloads. Discovery and transfers stop when you cancel, leave the screen or put the app in the background. The operating system asks for Bluetooth, nearby-device or local network access. Some operating-system versions also require location permission for these connections; Stacksmith does not request your geographic coordinates.
  • Nearby games start only when you choose Host game or Join nearby in Play. They use the same encrypted Nearby Connections technology and connection permissions. After both phones verify the connection code, connected players receive the game's player names, commander choices, life totals, counters and seat assignments. These are shared only with the connected group, without a Stacksmith game server. Reconnection identifiers are temporary and are not used for advertising. The host saves the current game locally; joining does not replace your own saved game. Connections stop when you leave or put the app in the background. An active game may reconnect when you return to this screen, with connection verification and a game update before you can make changes again.
  • Receiving a collection checks the file and shows a preview before you choose to replace this device's collection. A verified recovery copy of the current collection is saved on this device before replacement. Recovery files contain the same private information as a backup and are not encrypted by Stacksmith. They are excluded from the app's automatic operating-system backups and leave the app only when you choose Save a copy and a sharing destination. Transfer does not enable ongoing synchronization or automatic collection uploads.

What the app sends over the internet, and when

Offline copies of previously loaded news, set and sealed-product data, public deck and combo results, and card/product images stay on your device. They let the app display saved content when a connection is unavailable. The date of the last update is saved; viewing saved data does not upload your collection.

Every internet request carries standard connection information, such as your IP address. Beyond that, the app sends only what is listed here.

  • Nearby Connections diagnostics: Google's SDK documents collection of connection performance and device information, including model, country, build version and app package name, to improve the SDK. This is separate from the deck or collection file transferred between devices. On Android, Google documents a Usage & diagnostics device setting for controlling this reporting. See Google's Nearby Connections disclosure.
  • Expo EAS Update (u.expo.dev and Expo's asset servers): on app launch, checks for compatible app fixes and downloads their code and images. Requests include the operating-system platform, app runtime version, update channel, update identifiers, and a per-installation EAS client identifier. They also include update failure information, including an error message after a fatal update failure. Expo receives your IP address. Your collection and camera frames are not uploaded for app updates. A downloaded update takes effect on a later app restart.
  • Feedback: Send feedback in Settings opens our feedback page in your browser with Japanese or English selected to match the app. No collection or device details are added to the link. The page sends the feedback and screenshots you choose to submit. You may optionally provide a Twitter/X username to request a public @mention when your suggestion or fix is implemented; that username is included in the feedback email and issue and used for that announcement. The crash screen's Contact support opens an email draft, which is sent only when you send it.
  • Scryfall (scryfall.com) provides card data, set lists, card images, TCGplayer and Cardmarket prices, and format legality. Requests send the IDs of the relevant cards and printings, not your quantities, folders or other personal information. Requests are made as follows:
    • Upcoming sets: Home requests its set list from Scryfall only if the Cloudflare Pages file described below is unavailable.
    • TCGplayer Market and Cardmarket prices: daily prices normally come from Cloudflare Pages. Stacksmith requests prices from Scryfall when you tap Refresh prices in Settings, or when the price file has been unavailable for more than two days. It also requests prices for your printings while waiting for the first price-file download after you change price source, install an app update with new card data, or restore a backup. With these price sources, scanning a card sends nothing.
    • TCGplayer Low and Mid prices: Stacksmith asks Scryfall about each printing the first time it prices it, to find the matching TCGplayer listing. For printings it cannot price from TCGplayer's lists, it requests prices each day and when you scan, add or open a card.
    • Format legality: if the weekly card facts file does not yet include a card you own or open in a card or deck view, Stacksmith requests its legality from Scryfall.
    • New card data: downloads start when you choose to update. Sets available on Cloudflare Pages come from there; other sets come from Scryfall.
  • tcgcsv.com: only if you choose TCGplayer Low or Mid prices in Settings. The app downloads TCGplayer's public price lists for the sets your cards come from, and the list for a card's set when you scan, add or open that card. Apart from standard connection information, these requests reveal only which sets' price lists are downloaded.
  • Card Kingdom (api.cardkingdom.com): only when you choose Card Kingdom cash buylist prices in Settings, the app downloads its complete public singles price list, at most once a day automatically and when you tap Refresh prices. The request does not identify cards you own, quantities, folders or any other collection data. The list and its date stay on your phone. Cash values include only cards the store is currently buying; they are estimates for English Near Mint cards, subject to the store's grading and buying limits. They are separate from retail purchase budgets.
  • Choosing one Japanese shop in Settings downloads that shop's existing public card and sealed price files from Stacksmith's Cloudflare Pages site, instead of the merged shop file. These requests reveal the selected shop and your IP address, and do not send your collection. Missing prices are not substituted with another shop or market.
  • Hide prices is a local display preference. It hides displayed values, totals and price charts, and omits prices from spoken scan confirmations. Files you export and backups still contain the original amounts.
  • TCGplayer's image server (tcgplayer-cdn.tcgplayer.com) supplies photos for products shown in the Sealed tab's lists, search results and product pages. Requests use each product's TCGplayer number. The photos are then saved on your phone. These requests reveal which product photos you view.
  • European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu): Stacksmith downloads public daily exchange rates when it needs to convert prices. No information about you or your collection is included in the request beyond the standard connection information described above. Rates are downloaded when:
    • You select a display currency different from your price source's currency, in Settings or in Scan options for the current session.
    • Your collection includes a card priced in another market's currency. Japanese-language cards and cards in European languages are priced in the market for that language, then displayed in your market's currency.
    • You use Japan as your price source and a card has no Japanese shop listing, so its TCGplayer price is converted to yen.
  • Commander Spellbook (commanderspellbook.com): combos.
    • When you open a deck, its card names and counts are sent to show its combos and bracket.
    • When you open "Combos you could build", or use the deck builder or Suggest, the names and counts of the cards in your collection are sent.
    • A card's detail sheet sends that one card's name.
    • Nothing is sent to Commander Spellbook until you open one of these.
  • Archidekt (archidekt.com) provides Discover and Search in the Decks tab. Stacksmith sends your search criteria: format, commander, card name, colors, bracket and deck name. It downloads decks in the results to show how much of each you own, as well as decks you open or update. For a pasted Archidekt link, it sends only the deck ID from that link, and only when you open the deck or import its list. Your collection is not sent to Archidekt. Ownership comparisons happen on your phone.
  • Deck links: when you paste a supported deck link into Import and tap Fetch, Stacksmith requests the deck from the relevant site: Scryfall (api.scryfall.com), Deckstats (deckstats.net), MTGTop8 (mtgtop8.com), Aetherhub (aetherhub.com), ManaBox (cloud.manabox.app), TCGplayer (infinite-api.tcgplayer.com) or Deckbox (deckbox.org). It sends only the deck ID from the link, and only to that site. The returned deck list stays on your phone. Moxfield, TappedOut and MTGGoldfish links do not trigger a request; you need to paste those deck lists manually.
  • News on Home comes from a public feed selected by app language: MTGGoldfish (mtggoldfish.com) for English, German and Italian; Hareruya (article.hareruyamtg.com) for Japanese; and Cards Realm (mtg.cardsrealm.com) for French, Spanish and Portuguese. The request reveals your IP address and the selected language feed. No other information is sent.
  • Cloudflare Pages (stacksmith-app.pages.dev) hosts the public files below. App versions released before 3 October 2026 use GitHub Pages (colinwithat-boop.github.io) for the same files. Each request reveals your IP address and which file or language pack is requested. Your collection is not uploaded.
    • Daily card prices: a small file identifying the latest prices is checked a few times a day. Prices include every printing's TCGplayer Market and Cardmarket prices, using Scryfall data. Once a day, the app downloads changes since the previous day (about 1.5 MB). It downloads the full price file (about 3.5 MB) if it lacks the previous day's data, when you tap Refresh prices, and after you download new card data, change price source, install an app update with new card data, or restore a backup.
    • Japanese prices: when Japan is your price source, or your collection includes a Japanese-language copy, the app downloads a file of prices in yen from Hareruya and Bigweb (about 2.5 MB). The site gathers these from public shop listings daily; the app downloads the file once a day if it has changed. It also downloads the shops' sealed-product prices for the Sealed tab, and a small file of Rakuten and Yahoo! Shopping offers gathered through those services.
    • Coming up on Home: opening Home downloads a small file with upcoming sets, their names in the app's language, and Secret Lair drops. Card images come from Scryfall. If the file is unavailable and the app is not in English, it downloads a separate small file of translated set names.
    • Card facts: a file containing each card's format legality and colors (about 1 MB) is downloaded about once a week.
    • Sealed products: small files describe pack odds, the contents of boxes, bundles and decks, and their TCGplayer and Cardmarket prices. The site builds these from MTGJSON data, TCGplayer prices through tcgcsv.com, and Cardmarket's public price guide. Files are downloaded when you open Sealed, including one for each set you open, search for or reach while scrolling through older products.
    • New sets: when Scan or Check now in Settings finds new cards, the app downloads a small list of available sets to show the download size. The card data and scanner recognition data are downloaded when you choose to update.
    • Card-language packs: these contain names, and rules text where available, for cards printed in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Korean or Russian. Adding a card language in Settings downloads its pack. You are also prompted to download a missing pack when you choose that scan language in Settings or Scan, or select it for a card in card details or when adding a card by name. A pack downloads automatically for your phone's language, or when you switch the app to one of these languages, unless you previously removed it. An installed pack downloads again if an app update or restored backup leaves it missing. Opening Settings, Scan's language menu or a card's language list downloads the list of available packs and sizes.
  • Reading the text on cards happens on your phone: Google ML Kit on Android, Apple's Vision on an iPhone. The camera image and the text read are not sent anywhere.
    • On Android, like every app that uses ML Kit, it sends Google device information (such as the manufacturer, model and OS version), the app's name and version, a per-installation identifier, and performance and configuration details (such as how long recognition took), which Google uses for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
    • On an iPhone, Vision is part of iOS and the app sends nothing for it.
  • Updated card translations are downloaded from the app’s Cloudflare Pages site on launch, on returning to the app, and after adding new card data. English keyword data and Japanese names, rules text, type lines and picture references are kept current, along with your app language and installed card languages. Automatic downloads and writes wait until scanning is out of view. Requests identify the bundled database version, the requested language and the file version, and reveal your IP address. No collection, card names, quantities or other personal data are sent.
  • Links you tap (to Scryfall, TCGplayer, Cardmarket, MTGGoldfish, Hareruya, Bigweb, Rakuten, Yahoo! Shopping, Cards Realm, Archidekt, Commander Spellbook or Wizards' Secret Lair store) open in your browser. The Buy menu offers TCGplayer, Cardmarket, Hareruya and Bigweb. When you choose TCGplayer, Buy missing cards includes the names and quantities of missing cards from your deck or set in the link. Buy on your wishlist includes the names and quantities from that list. Choosing Hareruya for a list opens its HTTPS bulk-buy page inside Stacksmith and fills its form with the selected card names and quantities. Hareruya's page and scripts can read those form values. The merchant receives normal web connection information and may use cookies under its own policy. Stacksmith does not automatically submit the form, add cards to a cart or make a purchase. You control those actions on the merchant's page. The list is kept in memory for this screen, not placed in its URL or saved as browsing history by Stacksmith. An optional Copy list action writes the list to your system clipboard; the app does not read your clipboard.
  • Google Play or the App Store: to process the optional one-time Pro purchase and voluntary one-time tips. Stacksmith checks Pro ownership and uses transaction identifiers and payment status locally to finish tip purchases; tips do not unlock features. These transaction details are not uploaded to a Stacksmith server. Google or Apple handles payment under its own privacy policy; the app never sees your card or bank details.
  • Optional support and social links: the support panel in Settings opens Instagram, X or the developer's Ko-fi page only when you tap the corresponding link. Ko-fi lets you choose one-time or recurring support. These payments do not unlock features or change your Pro license. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle payment details and recurring payment management under their own policies. For Ko-fi contributions, Stacksmith does not receive payment details or confirm whether you contributed. Opening these sites reveals normal web connection information to them; no collection data or device identifier is added to the links. On iPhone, the panel checks the App Store account's storefront through the existing store library to determine whether to show the external payment link.
  • Share Stacksmith: after you tap Share, the app passes a short promotional message and Stacksmith's public download link to your operating system's share sheet. You choose the destination and whether to send or post it. No collection data, contacts or device identifiers are included. The download page is hosted on the existing Cloudflare Pages site and directs recipients to the appropriate app store.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No ads are displayed in the app.
  • No account, sign-in, email or contacts access.
  • No selling of data or sharing with Meta for ad measurement.
  • No advertising identifiers or install-attribution reports.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
